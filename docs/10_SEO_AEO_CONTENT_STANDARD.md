# SEO, AEO, and Content Standard

## SEO baseline

Server rendered crawlable HTML, correct status codes, canonical URLs, sitemap, robots.txt, metadata, structured data, internal links, accessibility, mobile performance, image text alternatives.

## AEO

AEO means making content clear, direct, well structured, entity consistent, useful, and source backed for both people and machine mediated answer systems.

## Content quality

No scaled near duplicate pages created only for keyword variants.
No fake authors, reviews, ratings, credentials, or claims.
No guaranteed ranking or AI citation claims.

## Publishing requirement

Every page must have a human or policy approved factual basis in the client knowledge system.

Visual and motion experimentation from `docs/27` must preserve heading hierarchy, crawlable important copy, descriptive links, canonical strategy, factual structured data, and accessible equivalents. Essential content must never exist only inside animation, canvas, image, or video.
