# Vector Product Vision and Positioning

## Purpose

Define what Vector is, who it serves, how it creates value, and what it must not become.

## Product

Vector is an Autonomous Growth Operating System operated commercially by Maximum Global Exposure. Each client receives a configured Vector Growth System.

## Commercial and technical split

Maximum Global Exposure and Vector are separate products in one commercial ecosystem.

- **Maximum Global Exposure** (`maxglobalexpo.com`) is the company and customer-acquisition brand. It sells services, explains Vector, generates MGE leads, publishes case studies, and directs clients to the application.
- **Vector** (`vector.maxglobalexpo.com` initially) is the operating platform. It owns clients, funnels, leads, email, social, SEO/AEO, analytics, experiments, agents, approvals, and automations.
- The MGE website is not the Vector application. Do not merge them into one product.
- Once Vector can operate production clients, MGE itself must become a Vector tenant so MGE is the first dogfood and reference implementation.
- Clients experience `theircompany.com`, not a Vector subdomain, except for temporary preview URLs. Vector stays invisible as infrastructure unless MGE brands the implementation as "Powered by Vector."
- A dedicated Vector domain may be introduced later if Vector becomes a standalone software brand.

Detailed hosting, scaling, and launch rules live in `26_MGE_VECTOR_HOSTING_SCALING_VECTOR24.md`.

## Core promise

Deploy a connected digital customer acquisition engine that can build funnels, acquire attention, capture and nurture leads, measure outcomes, and continuously improve within governed autonomy limits.

Mature-state commercial wording, only after operational proof:

> Your complete growth infrastructure launched in as little as 24 hours.

Required qualification:

> 24-hour launch begins once all required business information, access, approvals, and Vector Readiness requirements are complete.

Do not advertise a 24-hour guarantee from contract signing, or for highly regulated, complex ecommerce, large-migration, dedicated-infrastructure, or custom-enterprise clients.

Do not claim Vector guarantees revenue. It improves the probability and efficiency of generating, converting, and measuring demand (`docs/30`). Clients operate growth outcomes, not marketing infrastructure.

## Primary users

- MGE operators and strategists
- Client owners and marketing decision makers
- Client reviewers and analysts
- Automated Vector Agents acting under explicit policies

## North star

Qualified business outcomes attributable to Vector assisted growth activity.

## Supporting metrics

- Qualified leads
- Sales and revenue where available
- Conversion rate
- Cost per qualified lead
- Time from lead to response
- Percentage of routine work completed without human intervention
- Percentage of AI actions accepted without correction
- Experiment win rate
- Client retention
- Median time to Vector Ready
- Median Vector Ready to live
- Percentage of ready clients launched under 24 hours
- Manual interventions per launch versus launch defects

## Differentiation

Vector is not marketed as "we use AI." It is marketed as a dedicated autonomous growth engine with measurement and continuous improvement.

## Brand hierarchy

Maximum Global Exposure → company
Vector → platform
Vector Autonomous Growth OS → product category
Vector Growth System → one client implementation
Vector Intelligence → AI layer
Vector Agents → workers
Vector Insights → analytics and optimization
Vector Creative → client media ingest, generation, composition, approval, and distribution
Vector Insights / Outcomes → goals, sales outcomes, optional revenue, data health (`docs/30`)
Vector 24 → mature-state launch standard after readiness is complete

## Non goals

Do not become a generic page builder, mass SEO generator, universal CRM replacement, unrestricted autonomous agent, or clone based website factory.
Do not become a fork-per-client hosting company or a single physical-server architecture.

## Public experience

Strategic positioning must be immediately understandable on public pages: offer, audience, why it matters, next action.

`docs/27_VECTOR_FRONTEND_UI_UX_SALES_FUNNEL_STANDARD.md` translates positioning into narrative, hierarchy, proof, and conversion. It is a quality bar, not a single visual identity. Client brand and offer stay specific. The MGE marketing site, once operated as a Vector tenant, is governed by the same public-experience standard.

Vector product chrome — Control Plane and any future Vector software-marketing surface — uses `docs/28_VECTOR_HERO_AND_PRODUCT_VISUAL_LANGUAGE.md`. That identity must not become the default look of client sites.

Client campaign media — funnel imagery, social graphics, email banners, Open Graph images, and generated supporting visuals — is **Vector Creative**, governed by `docs/29_VECTOR_CREATIVE_ASSET_GENERATION_MEDIA_PIPELINE.md`. Vector is a complete growth operating system, not a copy-only automation tool.

Authenticated client experience — Today, goals, approvals, sales outcomes, and business-language reporting — is governed by `docs/30_VECTOR_CLIENT_EXPERIENCE_REVENUE_INTELLIGENCE_GROWTH_OUTCOMES.md`. Those screens use `docs/28` chrome. They are not a second Delivery site.
