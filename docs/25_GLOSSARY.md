# Glossary

**Vector** — Autonomous Growth OS.

**Vector Growth System** — One client's configured instance.

**Vector Intelligence** — AI reasoning and generation layer.

**Vector Agent** — Specialized bounded AI worker.

**Vector Insights** — Analytics and optimization capability.

**Control Plane** — Authenticated operations, configuration, governance, and reporting layer.

**Delivery Plane** — Public serving and execution layer.

**Tenant** — A client whose business data is isolated.

**Autonomy Level** — Permission class defining what an agent may do without human approval.

**Learning Object** — Validated record connecting context, action, outcome, and confidence.

**Provider Adapter** — Vector owned abstraction around an external service.

**Vector 24** — Mature-state standard: Vector Ready to a live initial growth system within 24 hours, excluding client-caused pauses.

**Vector Ready** — Calculated readiness status that starts the Vector 24 clock. Missing critical requirements block the SLA.

**Launch Class** — Complexity band (A–D) that sets internal build-time expectation after readiness.

**Launch State** — Auditable client launch status such as `onboarding`, `vector_ready`, `generating`, `qa`, `live`, or `paused`.

**Preview Hostname** — Private pre-launch URL for a client site. Must be `noindex` and must not publish production email or social.
